How it runs

Four stages. No surprises on the invoice.

Fixed scope, fixed price, weekly builds you can actually click on.

01

Scope

You send the brief, we ask whatever is missing in writing, and back comes a scope: deliverables, timeline, owner for each item, and what it costs. Nothing starts before you sign it off.

02

Design

Wireframes, visual direction and copy approved before a single line of code or a frame of footage is cut.

03

Build

Launch, then hosting, monitoring, content, ads and reporting on a monthly retainer if you want us to stay.
